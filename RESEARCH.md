# Research

Cross-project research for FXD is cataloged in the central private research library:

**`DumpsterFireWorks/Projects-research--library-`**

Use the library to discover reusable CAD, fixture-engineering, manufacturing, AI-design, architecture, audit, feasibility, benchmark, and experiment findings across projects.

## Authority boundary

- This repository remains authoritative for current FXD code, project status, product decisions, active gates, and original research artifacts.
- The research library is the cross-project discovery and preservation index. Cataloging research there does not activate implementation here.
- Original source paths and commits remain authoritative provenance.

## Cleanup rule

Before archiving or deleting this repository or any research-bearing branch, issue, PR, experiment, or document, verify that reusable research has been cataloged in `Projects-research--library-`.

Do not move customer/employer CAD, credentials, secrets, private runtime data, or sensitive evidence into the research library.
